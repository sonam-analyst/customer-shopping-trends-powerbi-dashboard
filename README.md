# Customer Shopping Trends 2023 – Power BI Dashboard

## 📊Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** created using the **Customer Shopping Trends 2023** dataset.

The dashboard analyzes customer shopping behavior and purchase patterns through interactive visualizations covering **purchase amounts, products, sizes, customer locations, review ratings, shipping types, previous purchases, and payment methods**.

##  Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform customer shopping data into meaningful visual insights and demonstrate how **Power BI and DAX** can be used to analyze customer behavior and support data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides insights into:

* **Purchase Amount by Size** – comparison of purchase amounts across M, L, S, and XL sizes.
* **Purchase Amount by Item** – analysis of purchase amounts for individual items such as Blouse, Shirt, Dress, Pants, Jewelry, Sunglasses, Belt, Scarf, Sweater, and Shorts.
* **Customer Location** – distribution of customers across different locations.
* **Average Review Rating** – analysis of customer review ratings.
* **Shipping Type Analysis** – comparison of previous purchases and purchase amounts across different shipping types.
* **Payment Method Analysis** – analysis of customer payment methods including Bank Transfer, Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, and PayPal.

The dashboard shows a total purchase amount of **$233,081** across the displayed items.

## Key Dashboard Insights

* **Blouse** has the highest displayed purchase amount at **$10,410**, followed by Shirt and Dress.
* Purchase amounts are compared across four sizes: **M, L, S, and XL**.
* The dashboard includes customer location analysis for locations such as **Montana, California, Idaho, and Illinois**.
* Shipping analysis compares **Standard, Free Shipping, Express, 2-Day Shipping, Store Pickup, and Next Day Air**.
* Payment methods displayed include **Bank Transfer, Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card, and PayPal**.
